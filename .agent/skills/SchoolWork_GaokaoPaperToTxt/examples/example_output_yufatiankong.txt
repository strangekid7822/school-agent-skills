【语法填空·习题】

An exhibition at the Jiushi Art Museum in Shanghai is featuring artwork inspired by Go, or weiqi in Chinese, ___56___ originated in China more than 4 000 years ago.
Go is one of ___57___ earliest binary-based (基于二元的) games. The movements of the black and white pieces reflect the basic ideas of Eastern philosophy, according to Tu Ningning, who is in charge of the exhibition.
"The exhibition brings together Go culture, cutting-edge technology and contemporary art," says Tu. "We hope ___58___ (present) the rather abstract Go game and AI in a visual context, and initiate dialogues with minimalist art, conceptual art and expressionism."
"In a Go game, each move should serve a long term goal. You try to lead the opponent into your trap and force them to follow your '___59___ (guide)' till they lose," explains Wang Wei, a Go player among the visitors to the exhibition.
"The players' personalities ___60___ (reveal) during the game, and one's weaknesses are exposed to the opponent," she adds. "A decent winner always ___61___ (try) to beat the opponent ___62___ no more than one or two points as a gesture (姿态) of respect for the other side."
Tu says that the balance between the black and white pieces, the beauty in the ___63___ (strategy) placement of the pieces, ___64___ the energy flow following each move inspired artists to create oil paintings, sculptures, ___65___ (digital) generated pictures and silk-screen prints for the exhibition.

【语法填空·答案解析】

56.which  考查定语从句 【解析】分析句子结构可知，空格处引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是Go，指物，关系词在从句中作主语，应用关系代词which引导该从句。故填which。
57.the  考查冠词 【解析】此处表示围棋是最早的基于二元的游戏之一。形容词最高级earliest前应用定冠词the。故填the。
58.to present  考查非谓语动词 【解析】hope to do sth.为固定搭配，意为"希望做某事"。故填to present。
59.guidance  考查词性转换和名词的单复数 【解析】分析句子结构可知，形容词性物主代词your后应用名词。此处指围棋对弈中的策略性引导行为，应用不可数名词guidance。故填guidance。
【知识点拨】guide作名词时，强调具体的"指导工具或人"如：a travel guide（旅游指南）、a tour guide（导游）。guidance作名词时，强调抽象的"指导行为"或"指导过程"，通常指向建议、方向或支持。
60.are revealed  考查动词的时态、语态和主谓一致 【解析】根据下文中的and和are exposed to可知，此处and连接并列句，陈述一般性的客观情况，应用一般现在时；主语The players' personalities与动词reveal之间是被动关系，应用被动语态；主语The players' personalities为复数，be动词应用are。故填are revealed。
61.tries  考查动词的时态和主谓一致 【解析】根据时间状语always及语境可知，此处陈述一般性的客观情况，时态应用一般现在时；主语A decent winner为第三人称单数，谓语动词应用第三人称单数形式。故填tries。
62.by  考查介词 【解析】此处表示以不超过一两分的优势战胜对手。by（表示数量、程度等）以……之差。故填by。
63.strategic(al)  考查词性转换 【解析】分析句子结构可知，此处应用形容词修饰名词placement。strategy的形容词形式为strategic(al)。故填strategic(al)。
64.and  考查连词 【解析】分析句子结构可知，"黑白棋子之间的制衡""棋子战略布局中的美感"和"每一步落子间流动的气韵"为that引导的宾语从句中的并列主语，应用连词and连接。故填and。
65.digitally  考查词性转换 【解析】分析句子结构可知，此处应用副词修饰动词generated，表示数字生成的图像和丝网印刷品。digital的副词形式为digitally。故填digitally。
