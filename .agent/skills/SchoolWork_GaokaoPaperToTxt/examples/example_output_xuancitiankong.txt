选词填空_暴风雨后的邻里互助
========================================

【选词填空·词库】

heavily    closer    felt    order    putting    mess

【选词填空·习题】

Ben could hear strong winds outside his home in Alabama. Black clouds were making the sky very dark. With no light outside, it ___61___ like midnight. The news on TV reported that a heavy rainstorm was in the area. Ben's dad was ___62___ pieces of wood over the windows while his mom was making sure the flashlights and radio were working. Ben was helping his mom make dinner when the rain began to beat ___63___ against the windows.

Ben finally fell asleep when the wind was dying down at around 3:00 a.m. When he woke up, the sun was rising. He went outside with his family and found the neighborhood in a ___64___. Fallen trees, broken windows and rubbish were everywhere. They joined the neighbors to help clean up the neighborhood together. Although the storm broke many things apart, it brought families and neighbors ___65___ together.

【选词填空·答案解析】

61. felt
【解析】空后有like midnight，表示“感觉像午夜”，应用felt like。

62. putting
【解析】句意为“爸爸正在把木板钉/放在窗户上”。空前有was，应用现在分词putting，构成过去进行时。

63. heavily
【解析】此处修饰beat against the windows，表示雨重重地拍打窗户，应用副词heavily。

64. mess
【解析】根据后文Fallen trees, broken windows and rubbish were everywhere可知，社区一片狼藉。in a mess意为“一团糟”。

65. closer
【解析】句意为“暴风雨让家人和邻居更加亲近”。bring ... closer together意为“使……更加团结/亲近”。

【选词填空·重点词汇】

strong winds - 强风
outside - adv./prep. 在外面；在……外面
make sure - 确保
flashlight - n. 手电筒
radio - n. 收音机
beat against - 敲打，拍打
fall asleep - 入睡
die down - 逐渐减弱，逐渐平息
at around - 大约在
wake up - 醒来
neighborhood - n. 街区，社区
fallen - adj. 倒下的
rubbish - n. 垃圾
clean up - 打扫，清理
break apart - 破裂，分裂
bring ... together - 使……团结起来
feel like - 感觉像
put ... over - 把……覆盖/放在……上
in a mess - 一团糟
closer - adv. 更接近地；adj. 更亲近的
