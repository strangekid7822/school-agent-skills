示例题_补全对话
========================================

【补全对话·习题】

A: Hi, Mary. What are you doing now?
B: Hi, John. I am watching a video.
A: (41)___________________?
B: It's about the Belt and Road（一带一路）. (42)___________________?
A: Yes, I've just heard something about that on the radio. (43)___________________?
B: It is great. In the video, the conversation between the father and the daughter explains the topic clearly. (44)___________________?
A: Yes, I'd like to. But I also want to watch another program called Readers.
B: Why not watch Readers after this one?
A: (45)___________________.

【补全对话·答案解析】

41. What is it about?
【解析】询问视频内容的常用句式。B随即回答"It's about the Belt and Road."，说明此处需填内容询问句。亦可用 What's it about?

42. Have you heard about it / that?
【解析】B介绍完主题后转问A是否了解，A答"Yes, I've just heard something about that."，因此此处应是询问对方是否听说过。亦可用 Do you know anything about it?

43. What do you think of it?
【解析】A表示听说过后，自然追问B对视频的看法，B随即评价"It is great."，因此此处填征求意见的问句。亦可用 How do you like it? / How do you feel about it?

44. Would you like to watch it with me?
【解析】B介绍完视频后发出邀请，A回答"Yes, I'd like to."接受邀请，因此此处应填邀请句。亦可用 Do you want to watch it together?

45. Good idea. / That sounds great.
【解析】B建议先看完这个再看Readers，A应表示同意。此处填表示赞同的短句。亦可用 OK. / Sure.

【补全对话·重点词汇】

Belt and Road - 一带一路（专有名词，固定说法）
hear about - 听说，了解到
on the radio - 通过广播
What is it about - （询问主题的固定句式）这是关于什么的？
What do you think of - 你觉得……怎么样？
explain - v. 解释，说明
clearly - adv. 清楚地
would you like to - 你愿意……吗？（礼貌邀请句式）
Why not do sth. - 为何不……？（提建议的固定句式）
That sounds great - 听起来不错（表示赞同）
